# HazBIM Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026

HazBIM is developed and supported by Eng. Hazem El-Husieny. This policy explains the information used by the HazBIM Revit add-in for licensing, offline access, updates, and support.

## Information HazBIM processes

When you sign in, HazBIM processes the username and password supplied for your HazBIM account, a device identifier derived from the current Windows user security identifier, the application version, and temporary authentication/session values. The licensing service may return and HazBIM may store account status information such as user rank, registration date, subscription expiration, lifetime-license status, and the last successful online verification time.

HazBIM does not intentionally collect the contents of your Revit models, drawings, exported files, or schedules. Tool operations process project information locally to perform the commands you request.

## How the information is used

The information is used to:

- authenticate trial and licensed accounts;
- enforce account, device, subscription, and offline-access rules;
- maintain a signed-in session and diagnose licensing failures;
- check whether a compatible HazBIM update is available; and
- respond to support, trial, purchase, and account-deletion requests.

HazBIM does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

## Local storage

If you choose to sign in, HazBIM stores the supplied username and password in the HazBIM license file on the computer so it can sign in automatically. That credentials file is stored as plain JSON next to the installed add-in files. Protect access to the Windows account and computer where HazBIM is installed.

After a successful online sign-in, HazBIM may store an encrypted, integrity-protected offline license cache containing the username, device identifier, account status, last verification time, and license expiration information. HazBIM also stores the selected update channel locally. Authentication diagnostic logs may be written locally when a login succeeds or the licensing service returns an unexpected response.

You can remove locally stored HazBIM information by clearing the credentials and deleting the HazBIM data files. Uninstall or upgrade operations may preserve license and settings files, so contact support if you want help identifying and removing the relevant installation and application-data files.

## Network services and third parties

HazBIM sends authentication requests to the licensing service hosted at `eauth.us.to`. The service receives the authentication and device information described above. Its infrastructure providers may also receive normal connection data such as IP address and request time.

HazBIM checks GitHub-hosted release files for updates. GitHub may receive normal connection information, including IP address, request time, HazBIM version in the request user agent, and the requested release-file address. Opening Help, Privacy Policy, update-download, LinkedIn, email, or other external links uses the selected browser or email application and is subject to the destination provider's privacy practices.

Third-party providers process information only for the services described here and are expected to protect it under their applicable terms and privacy obligations.

## Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the computer until it is cleared, deleted, replaced, or removed during uninstall. Server-side licensing records are retained while needed to provide trial or paid access, maintain account and transaction records, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and meet legal obligations.

To request correction or deletion of a HazBIM account or associated licensing information, email [hazem.r.elhusieny@gmail.com](mailto:hazem.r.elhusieny@gmail.com). Some limited records may be retained where required for security, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal compliance. Deleting licensing records may end access to HazBIM.

## Your choices

You may decline to provide account credentials, but licensed HazBIM commands will not be available. You may request account deletion, clear saved credentials, remove local HazBIM data, or uninstall the add-in. Uninstalling may preserve license and settings data unless those files are removed separately. You can select the Stable or Beta update channel in HazBIM's update window.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when HazBIM features, providers, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
